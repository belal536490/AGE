FAQ

Common questions

Can this download YouTube videos?

No. The app does not extract or bypass YouTube video streams. It embeds YouTube videos for viewing and links to the official video page.

What can I download?

Direct video file URLs that you own, created, licensed, or have permission to download.

Does the app store my links?

No. It runs in your browser and does not send URLs to a backend server.
